THE PAIN

The market is talking. Most content systems cannot hear it.

Searches, comments, saves, store visits, and purchases reveal what people are trying to solve. In most teams, those signals never change the next piece of content.

“Most AI writes from a prompt. Molly starts from how the market behaves.”

RELIABILITY PROOF

PRODUCTION CONTROL

The decision can travel safely into production.

The market loop is useful only when its evidence, generation, recovery, publishing, and readback remain accountable. The public view exposes the control logic, not the proprietary runtime.

  1. 01ObserveCollect evidence
  2. 02ContractSeal the task
  3. 03GenerateProduce a candidate
  4. 04GateEvaluate release
  5. 05RecoverRetry one node
  6. 06DeliverVerify readback
A

Evidence grounding

Accepted results remain traceable to their source material and task boundary.

B

Multimodal evaluation

Visual integrity and deliverable completeness are checked before release.

C

Job isolation

Each run receives an isolated evidence bundle to reduce cross-task contamination.

D

Scoped recovery

A failed node is retried without silently accepting degradation or restarting everything.

E

Human authority

The agent can recommend and execute, but it cannot manufacture approval.

F

Operational readback

Completion depends on verified downstream state, with evidence from the real destination.
